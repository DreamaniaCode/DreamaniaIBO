Does Dreamania Player include any built-in TV channels? +
No. Dreamania Player is a pure, high-performance media player. It does not provide or host any media streams. You must enter your own legal IPTV playlist credentials via Xtream Codes API or M3U playlist format.
How do I add channels to my Favorites? +
While in the channel list, select any channel and press the Red button or Long-Press the OK button on your remote. The heart indicator turns red (❤️), and the channel will automatically appear in your pinned "❤️ Favorites" category at the top!
How do I reorder or move channels in the list? +
Select a channel and press the Yellow button on your TV remote. A convenient modal appears allowing you to move the channel Up, Down, Move to Top, or enter interactive remote mode to position the channel wherever you prefer.
Can I switch between multiple audio languages or subtitles? +
Yes! While streaming, press OK or Down on your TV remote to open the bottom player OSD. Select the Audio or Subtitles button to choose from all available Dolby Surround tracks and language commentaries.
